# Data Availability

**Paper:** Learning and the Efficiency of Physician Referrals

**QJE data policy:** QJE requires that replication materials (data, programs, and computational details sufficient for replication) be deposited to the QJE Dataverse (Harvard Dataverse), with a Readme documenting each file and the replication steps. This requirement applies **at acceptance**, prior to publication, not at initial submission. At submission, QJE requires only a cover-letter note when the underlying data are proprietary or restricted; that note is included in `cover-letter.tex`.

## Data sources

- **Medicare 100% fee-for-service claims (Part A and Part B), 2008--2018** --- restricted-use. Obtained from the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) under a Data Use Agreement and accessed within the CMS Virtual Research Data Center (VRDC). Not redistributable by the authors. Available to qualified researchers through the standard application process administered by the Research Data Assistance Center (ResDAC, https://resdac.org).
- **Medicare Beneficiary Summary Files (MBSF)** --- restricted-use CMS data, obtained and accessed under the same CMS Data Use Agreement.
- **Medicare Data on Provider Practice and Specialty (MD-PPAS)** --- restricted-use CMS data, obtained and accessed under the same CMS Data Use Agreement.
- **American Hospital Association (AHA) Annual Survey** --- proprietary; available by license from the AHA (https://www.ahadata.com). Not redistributable by the authors.
- **Provider of Services (POS) files** --- public; available from CMS (https://www.cms.gov/data-research).

## Replication statement

Upon acceptance, all analysis code and the permissible public data inputs will be deposited to the QJE Dataverse with documentation sufficient for replication. The restricted-use CMS data and the proprietary AHA Annual Survey cannot be shared by the authors, but the analysis is fully reproducible for any researcher who has obtained the CMS data through the standard ResDAC application process and has licensed the AHA Annual Survey.
